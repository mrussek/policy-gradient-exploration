Why does company quality matter for bonds?
A higher credit rating means lower default risk, which allows AMC to issue bonds at a tighter (lower) yield spread over U.S. Treasuries. This directly affects the discount rate we use in our DCF model.

Step 5 of 6

Discounting Cash Flows to Present Value

We discount each future cash flow back to today using the YTM. The DCF formula for a single payment is:

PVt = CFt ÷ (1 + r/m)t
where r = annual YTM, m = periods per year, t = period number
